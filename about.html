Our story

Food that tastes like someone cared

Pakaoo started with a simple observation: the best meals in Bengaluru aren't in fancy kitchens — they're in someone's home.

Why we exist

Delivery apps optimised for speed gave us convenience but took away the soul of a meal. We built Pakaoo to do the opposite — start with the food, and make the logistics serve it.

Every partner kitchen is one we've visited, tasted, and trusted. We keep the menu small on purpose, so each dish gets the attention it deserves.

What we believe

  • Fresh beats fast. Cooked to order, never reheated.
  • Local is better. We source within the city whenever we can.
  • People over process. Our cooks and riders are partners, not cogs.
